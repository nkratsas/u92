Music recommendation: 

If you’re sick of your playlist, looking for a new artist, or you like indie artists similar to Noah Kahan, you should check out Jonah Kagen. 

  
Jonah Kagen has about 2 million monthly listeners on Spotify and for good reason. Two of my top picks include his new release “The Road,” and “broken,” from his EP georgia got colder. 

  
These are the perfect songs to add to your fall playlist and listen to while waiting for the weather to get colder and the leaves to change.

West Virginia University Women’s Soccer (6-2-2) will host the University of Arizona Wildcats (7-4) at Dick Dlesk Soccer Stadium on Thursday, Oct. 2 at 7 p.m.

  

This will be the Mountaineers' first home conference matchup of the 2025 campaign. Last  
season, they only lost one Big 12 affair at DDSS and ended third in the conference with a record  
of 8-2-1. The Wildcats finished in 7th with a conference record of 6-4-1. The two teams lone Big  
12 draw came against each other in their only meeting of the season in Tempe, AZ.  
  

WVU has played four straight games on the road and is just returning from a trip to Utah, where  
they beat BYU 3-2 and drew 1-1 with the University of Utah. The Mountaineers currently sit at 1-  
0-2 in Big 12 play.  
  

During their win in Provo, senior forward Taylor White was credited with the game-winning goal,  
which marked her team-leading fourth score of the season as well as the 20th tally of her  
Mountaineer career.  
  

In Salt Lake City, the night started with a scoreless first half, but the Utes got on the board at the  
56:42 mark. That lead didn't last long as West Virginia responded at 57:41 with a goal from  
junior midfielder Anna Hauer. To keep the score knotted, WVU’s junior goalkeeper Bailey  
Herfurth notched a career-best five saves in the contest, continuing a stellar season in the net.  
  

The Wildcats are coming off a 3-1 victory against Oklahoma State at Mulcahy Stadium in  
Tucson, A.Z. Freshman Mireya Stephenson and sophomore Marissa Arnst earned their first  
career goals to make the difference.[](https://wvusports.com/sports/womens-soccer/roster/taylor-white/19023 "https://wvusports.com/sports/womens-soccer/roster/taylor-white/19023")[](https://wvusports.com/sports/womens-soccer/roster/anna-hauer/19028 "https://wvusports.com/sports/womens-soccer/roster/anna-hauer/19028")Through 11 games this season, Arizona has given up the most goals in the Big 12 at 16. 

  

West Virginia has played one less game but has only conceded 7 scores. The teams both average  
1.8 points per game.  
  

The full matchup between WVU and UofA will be broadcast on U92 The Moose. Catch Nico  
Caretto on play-by-play and Brett Berner on color commentary.  
  

The pregame show will be hosted by R.J. Rice, who will be joined by Logan Powell, Ben  
Stautzenbach and Cody Burton, 

  

The Pregame show is scheduled for 6:30 p.m. Kickoff is set for 7:00 . The broadcast can be heard locally on 91.7 FM, u92themoose.com, an Alexa At-Home Device, and the TuneIn app on your mobile device.

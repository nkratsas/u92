U92 the Moose is conducting its first ever pledge drive. 

Dubbed "Pledge Your Love to the Moose" in honor of Valentine's Day, the pledge drive begins Monday, February 13th at 12 p.m. The 36-hour drive will wrap at 11:59 p.m. on Valentine's Day.

For more details, check out u92themoose.com/pledge.

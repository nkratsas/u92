MORGANTOWN, W.Va. — U92 Sports will broadcast its award-winning coverage of the NFL Draft on April 23 and 24. With the draft taking place nearby along Interstate 79, the U92 sports staff will provide coverage of every pick in the first and second rounds.

Sports Director Quinn Robie will host the draft show live from the Student Media Building. He will be joined by analysts Connor Roberts, Brett Berner and Matt Foley. Assistant Sports Director Nico Caretto will provide coverage from a general manager’s perspective, while Cody Burton will deliver social media updates.

Before the draft, Connor Roberts will host the pre-draft show, previewing potential storylines and selections. Analysts DJ McDermott, Brayden Laslo, Blake Fuchs and Logan Powell will join the coverage, with Aaron Pepling providing social media updates.

The pre-draft show will air from 6:30 to 7:30 p.m., followed by the draft show at 8 p.m., featuring pick-by-pick coverage through the end of the first round. Second-round coverage will begin at 7 p.m. on April 24.

Both shows will stream live on the U92 The Moose YouTube page.

U92’s coverage of the 2026 NFL Draft will also be available at U92theMoose.com, on 91.7 WWVU-FM in Morgantown, on the TuneIn app and on Alexa-enabled devices.

U92 staff will also host a reaction show on Twitch, led by DJ McDermott and members of the sports staff.

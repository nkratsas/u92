The now ranked No. 23 West Virginia Mountaineers (13-4) will host the Arizona State Sun Devils (10-7) on Tuesday.

  
In the Mountaineers last outing they had a thrilling upset victory over the then ranked No. 2 Iowa State Cyclones. A day celebrating the late great Jerry West saw head coach Darian DeVries add another victory to his already impressive list.

  
In addition to Iowa State, Coach DeVries now boasts victories over the Gonzaga Bulldogs and the Kansas Jayhawks (West Virginia's first victory at Phog Allen Fieldhouse).  
  

Javon Small had another amazing performance, posting 27 points, seven rebounds, five assists, and four steals, which led all Mountaineers in the game.

  
Javon Small and Darien DeVries continue to etch their names into West Virginia history and have turned around the entire state of the program in just 17 games.

  
Arizona State is currently on a four-game slide as it sits at a 10-7 record overall. Head coach Bobby Hurley has coached a well-rounded team this season as five Arizona State players average at least 10 points per game, being led by BJ Freemen who averages 13.2.

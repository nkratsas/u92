      No. 25 WVU Men’s Soccer is set for a midweek Sun Belt Conference clash
      with the UCF Knights. The Mountaineers enter the contest with a 6-2-4
      record and are second in the conference table at eight points.


      The Knights enter with a 6-3-3 record and are sixth in the Sun Belt with
      seven points. UCF defeated Old Dominion 5-1 at UCF Soccer and Track
      Complex Friday.<br>Forward Drew Hansen leads the Knights with seven goals
      and 15 total points.


      Midfielder Younes Boudinar leads the way with three assists.<br>Matisse
      Hebert has started every match in goal for UCF, picking up six clean
      sheets and 27 saves.


      WVU is led in goals by fourth-year forward Pablo Pozos with four.
      Third-year midfielder Isaac Scheer leads the team with three assists.
      Third-year Toby Mawer has started all but two matches in goal this season,
      picking up five clean sheets and 17 total saves.


      U92 will provide live coverage for the game. Richard Turkovic will be
      providing play-by-play commentary and will be joined by color analyst
      Gavin Wheeler.


      The pre-, half- and post-game show will be hosted by Anthony Ritchie.
      Blake Fuchs and Crystal Hanes will be joining for analysis.


      Kickoff for the game is set for 7:00 p.m. at Dick Dlesk Stadium. Fans can
      listen in on WWVU 91.7FM or online at [u92themoose.com](u92themoose.com
      "u92themoose.com").

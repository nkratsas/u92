Hired last January, Dan Stratford expects to finally get a chance to see WVU's men's soccer program in live action after a long lay-off due to delays related to the COVID-19 global pandemic.

  

Stratford, who comes to WVU after a sterling three-year stint at Division II University of Charleston, sat down with Sr. U92 Soccer Producer Matt Drabble (virtually) for an interview. 

  

Stratford won two MEC titles and two national championships in Division II, sporting a 61-4-5 record over three seasons. 

  

The interview with the former Mountaineer standout can be heard Monday, February 15 at 9 a.m. and again at 3 p.m. on U92 the Moose.

  

U92 the Moose will air six WVU home matches on 91.7 FM, the station's terrestrial signal. All broadcasts can also be heard online at using the station's live stream at u92themoose.com.

  

**Schedule**

March 06, 1 p.m.  - WVU vs. Northern Illinois 

March 17, 3 p.m.  - WVU vs. Bowling Green State University

March 24, 7 p.m.  - WVU vs. Marshall

March 31, 7 p.m.  - WVU vs. Akron

April 04, 1 p.m.     - WVU vs. Western Michigan University

April 18, 12 p.m.   - WVU vs. Southern Illinois University-Edwardsville

  

Pre-game coverage of all broadcasts begins no later than 15 minutes before kickoff.

        ## **Who We Are**


        **U92 The Moose (WWVU-FM)** is West Virginia University’s independent,
        student-run college radio station. Broadcasting at 91.7 FM and streaming
        worldwide, we are the campus hub for new music, independent journalism,
        and live Mountaineer sports.


        More than just a radio station, U92 is a premier **experiential learning
        laboratory**. For over 40 years, we have provided WVU students with an
        elite, hands-on environment to launch their careers. Under the
        mentorship of professional staff and supported by a vast network of
        successful alumni, students don’t just observe—they do. Our volunteers
        and student directors manage daily business operations, engineer live
        broadcasts, produce digital content, and host on-air programming.


        &nbsp;


        ### What We Do


        * **Music & Culture:** Operating 24/7, our DJs program their own weekly
        shows with a focus on underexposed, alternative, and underground artists
        you won't hear on commercial radio. From local spotlights like
        *Morgantown Sound* to deep-dives into jazz, blues, and classical, we
        champion musical discovery and diverse voices.

        * **Sports Broadcasting:** Our sports department delivers live coverage
        of WVU baseball, men's and women's basketball, men's and women's soccer,
        and WV Black Bears Baseball. Students handle everything from
        play-by-play and color commentary to pre- and post-game production,
        building skills that have launched alumni directly into roles at major
        sports networks and professional leagues.

        * **News & Journalism:** The U92 newsroom provides regular updates and
        hard-hitting public affairs programming. Students supplement their
        classroom education with real-world field reporting, interviewing, and
        investigative writing.

      D. Eric Nuttall M.Ed., a broadcaster turned educator, is the Broadcast
      Adviser for WVU Student Media and Program Coordinator for U92 the Moose,
      WVU's award-winning campus radio station.


      At U92, Eric takes his 40 years of experience in radio, television and
      internet broadcasting to work directly with students aspiring to head into
      the broadcast industry or simply looking to be on the radio. Eric is
      excited to work with the staff of U92 and steer today's broadcasters into
      the field.


      Eric has over 40 years of radio experience, including WVCS, WLSW, WQTW,
      WMBS, WASP (AM/FM), WJPA (AM/FM), WLDJ, all in Western Pennsylvania. Prior
      to coming to U92, Eric was a teacher of Communications for 25 years at
      Albert Gallatin High School in Fayette County, PA, and helped to create
      and operate Fayette TV, a community cable TV station in Uniontown. He also
      hosts and produces the very successful podcast "The Bill Alexander Show,"
      where he has interviewed a multitude of celebrities.

      No. 24 WVU Men’s Soccer is looking to record its first win since September
      22nd. The Mountaineers are in a rough stretch, with it being four matches
      since the program recorded a win.


      West Virginia is coming off a draw against American University. In this
      game, Jacob Redenfors was one of the only playmakers in this game that had
      a shot on goal.


      The last time that the Mountaineers recorded a score was against the Old
      Dominion Monarchs, with defender Aurélien Cormier putting up the only
      score. This West Virginia program predominantly scores in the second half,
      making it very difficult at times to be able to put away games.


      WVU currently sits at a record of 5-2-4 and will play the Kentucky
      Wildcats on Friday, October 16th. This game will be a double header for
      Dick Dlesk Soccer Stadium, as the women’s team will be playing the UCF
      Knights.


      The Kentucky Wildcats are coming into Dick Dlesk with a 1-0 loss to the
      South Carolina Gamecocks. The Wildcat offense is led by Costa Iliadis, who
      has 2 goals along with 3 assists to lead the Wildcat squad.


      Kentucky has had a really rough season so far; the Wildcats have only
      recorded 1 win against the Old Dominion Monarchs.


      U92 will provide live coverage for the game. Cody Burton will be providing
      play-by-play commentary and will be joined by color analyst Conor
      Bratchford.


      The pre-, half-, and post-game show will be hosted by Anthony Ritchie.
      Crystal Hanes and Ethan Farrell will be joining Anthony to go over the
      highlights of the game.


      Kickoff for the game is set for 7:00 p.m. at Dick Dlesk Stadium. Fans can
      listen in on WWVU 91.7FM or online at u92themoose.com.

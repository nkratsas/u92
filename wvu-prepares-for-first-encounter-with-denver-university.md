West Virginia will host the Denver University Pioneers at Dick Dlesk Soccer Stadium on Friday, Sept. 11, at 7 p.m. This game will mark the first time in program history that these two teams have met, producing a unique story to see how these two teams line up.

The Mountaineers are coming off a comeback victory against the George Mason Patriots. In order to get the win, the playmakers Julen Recalde and Pablo Pozos contributed to take the score to 2-1.

Pozos and Recalde are the only players who have been productive for the Mountaineers offense so far this season. The two playmakers combine for 2 goals apiece within 3 games.

The Denver Pioneers are rolling into Morgantown looking for a fresh start after a 3-1 loss to the Indiana Hoosiers. The only goal that was recorded in this game was from defender Will King.

To pack a punch on this offense, the Pioneers rely on forward Bryce Willoughby and midfielder Dominic Kallay. Willoughby was a part of the Summit League First Team in his 2025 season, making him a key player for the Mountaineers to look out for.

The Pioneers are very versatile when it comes to the offense. They have 5 players on their roster who have recorded a goal this season, threatening any team that dares to stand in their way.

U92 will provide live coverage, with Cody Burton providing play-by-play commentary and Connor Roberts as the color analyst. The pregame, halftime, and postgame coverage will be hosted by RJ Rice along with Crystal Hanes, Anthony Ritchie, and Gavin Wheeler.

The stage is set and the curtain is about to reveal what these two programs have. Fans can listen in on WWVU 91.7FM or online at u92themoose.com.

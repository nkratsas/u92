Morgantown, W.Va. - The West Virginia Mountaineers men’s soccer team will face the  
Kentucky Wildcats on Friday, September 27, at Dick Dlesk Soccer Stadium in  
Morgantown, WV.

Last Saturday on September 21, West Virginia narrowly defeated the Old Dominion  
Monarchs by a score of 1-0 on the road. It was the first win for the Mountaineers after  
two straight draws against Loyola Marymount and UNC Greensboro. The Mountaineers  
have yet to lose a game this season.

Against Old Dominion, West Virginia was led by junior midfielder Ethan Dekel Daks,  
who scored his first goal of the season. Goalkeeper Marc Bonnaire recorded his fifth  
win of the season with three saves against the Monarchs.  
  
The Kentucky Wildcats have begun their season with an overall record of 1-2-3. They  
have only played one game on the road this season, where they tied against Lipscomb  
by a score of 2-2.  
  
The Wildcats are led this season by sophomore forward Isaiah Chisolm, who has two  
goals and four points this season. Goalkeeper Casper Mols has a 1.60 GAA and has  
not allowed more than two goals in a game this season.  
  
Since 2019, the Mountaineers have had a 0-3-1 record against the Wildcats. The last  
matchup between the two teams was in 2023, which ended in a 0-1 defeat for West  
Virginia. The Mountaineers will look to get their first ever win against the Wildcats on  
Friday.

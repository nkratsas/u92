The West Virginia Mountaineers will play against Utah this Sunday, Oct. 11. The Mountaineers are
currently on a 3-game winning streak with the hopes of continuing this momentum against the Utes.

The women’s soccer program is coming off a 2-0 win against a ranked BYU team. Within this game,
Sophia Nickel carried the boats for the Mountaineers and accounted for both of the goals recorded.

Nickel displays her ability to be that difference maker in the Stats. So far, she has led the team with 7
goals this season along with 1 assist.

Sophia Nickel is not the only player who contributes to this squad. Anna Hauer is also a crucial piece
to this team, leading with key assists to set the Mountaineers up for opportunities to score.

West Virginia is coached by Nikki Izzo Brown who is in her 32nd season of coaching with an
impressive tenure of having 29 winning seasons. With this record, Brown is known for her
consistency and continues to make her program well respected.

The Utah Utes are coming off a 1-0 loss to the Cincinnati Bearcats. This loss displays the struggles
that this program has had, as they have a record of 4-6-3.

The Utes are very versatile when it comes to their offense. They have three players who have all
recorded 3 goals so far this season.

Shannon Davidson is one of these key contributors, having 3 goals along with 3 assists to aid the
Utes' cause.

U92 will be providing live coverage for the game. Richard Turkovich will be providing the play-by-
play commentary along with Aaron Pepling as the color analyst.

The pre-, half-, and post-game show will be hosted by Blake Fuchs. Blake will be joined by Domenic
Brusco, John Doucette, and Ryan Boynes to provide some informational analysis of the game.

Kickoff for the game is set for 12:00 noon at Dick Dlesk Stadium. Fans can listen in on WWVU
91.7FM or online at u92themoose.com.

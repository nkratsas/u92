      No. 12 WVU Women’s Soccer is taking on UCF this Friday, Oct. 16, at Dick
      Dlesk Soccer Stadium in a Big 12 clash.


      UCF comes into the matchup with an 8-3-1 overall record. In the
      conference, they sit at No. 7 with a 3-2-1 conference record.


      Despite that, the Knights have not shied away in their ranked matchups,
      tying then-No. 19 Texas Tech and taking down then-No. 17 Colorado 2-0 just
      last week.


      Forward Naomi Clark leads the UCF offense, producing nine goals and two
      assists.


      The Mountaineers will look to extend their four-game winning streak to
      five on Friday in the last game of the homestand. WVU is 4-1-1 in the
      conference and currently second in the conference behind Texas Tech.


      No doubt their offensive charge will be led by third-year forward/defender
      Sophia Nickel, who has seven goals and one assist so far this season.


      Kickoff is slated for 3 p.m., and live coverage will be provided by the
      U92. Fans can listen to the game on WWVU 91.7 FM or online at
      [u92themoose.com](http://u92themoose.com).

      - title: What We Do
        text: >-
          U92 is a 24/7, student-powered radio station. Everything you hear —
          music, news, sports, talk shows, podcasts — is created by students.


          #### Music


          Our DJs play music you won’t hear on commercial radio. We spotlight
          new, under-the-radar artists and help shape the sound of college
          radio. You don’t need to be a music expert — just curious and
          open-minded.


          We also do specialty and theme shows. Whether you're into classic
          rock, jazz, or even show tunes, U92 has a place for you. We even do
          live shows with local bands and DJ events.


          [You can learn more about our Music Department here](/music/).


          #### Sports


          From football, basketball, soccer, and baseball, our sports department
          broadcasts games, produces talk shows and podcasts, and delivers
          regular updates. If you love sports and storytelling, this is where
          passion meets practice.


          We cover play-by-play of nearly every home game and select away games
          of men's and women’s basketball, men's and women’s soccer, baseball,
          football, and other select sports. We also have an exciting summer
          internship opportunity with the [WV Black
          Bears](/sports/wv-black-bears/).


          Beyond play-by-play, the sports department hosts pre- and post-game
          shows, talk shows, and other exciting sports programming like
          Wednesday night's Sports Night, the Touchdown City Tailgate, and a
          growing list of sports podcasts.


          [Learn more about Sports opportunities here](/sports/).


          #### News


          Our news team reports on campus and community stories, delivering
          updates and producing in-depth programs. It’s hands-on experience in
          reporting, interviewing, writing, and producing — the kind you can’t
          fully get in a classroom.


          #### Other Opportunities


          U92 isn't just about playing music or covering sports. We have lots of
          opportunities to be a part of our radio station.


          * **Digital Content**\- Help create content for our website,
          newsletter, or online zine, "Wireless," like writing sports recaps,
          music reviews, or just what's going on at the station.

          * **Social Media Team**\- Help create content for our social media
          accounts to promote the station.

          * **Podcasting**\- Podcasting isn't just for sports- if you have an
          idea for a podcast, U92 hasa place for you to do it.

          * [**Be the Moose**](/about-us/meet-zeus/)\- Ever dream of walking
          around campus in a big moose costume? We're always looking for people
          to be Zeus, our station mascot.

          * **Events**\- DJ or work at our events on campus, including weekly
          farmers markets, career fairs, student org fairs, and more.

          * **Production and Engineering**\- It takes an army to keep U92
          running. We're always looking for producers, board operators, audio
          editors, and even engineers.

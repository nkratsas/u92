MORGANTOWN, W.Va. — U92 is set to broadcast the Sun Belt Conference men’s soccer tournament for the third consecutive season.

U92 will cover West Virginia University’s games throughout the tournament. Games kick off Sunday, Nov. 9, at 3 p.m.

After earning the No. 2 seed in the Sun Belt Conference Tournament, West Virginia will host postseason play in Morgantown. The Mountaineers open tournament action against No. 7-seed Old Dominion at 3 p.m. Sunday, Nov. 9. No. 3-seed Marshall and No. 6-seed Georgia State will follow with a 7 p.m. matchup.

If West Virginia advances, it will face the winner of Marshall and Georgia State in the semifinals on Wednesday, Nov. 12, at 7 p.m.

U92 will provide full game coverage of the WVU men’s soccer quarterfinal. Sports Director Quinn Robie will handle play-by-play, joined by Conor Bratchford as color analyst.

Pregame coverage begins at 2:30 p.m., hosted by RJ Rice from the U92 studios. He will be joined by Ben Stautzenbach, Richard Turkovich, and Aaron Pepling.

The broadcast can be heard locally on 91.7 FM, online at u92themoose.com, on an Alexa at-home device, and via the TuneIn app.

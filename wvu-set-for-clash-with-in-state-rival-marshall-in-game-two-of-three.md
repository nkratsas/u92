WVU baseball is set to take on the Marshall University Thundering Herd in game two of a three-game series. 

The first time these two teams battled it out on the field was on March 24th, where WVU took the victory 3-0. 

West Virginia comes into the game in need of a win after dropping their last series to UCF. They come in with a record 21-7. WVU sits third in the Big 12 standings, behind Kansas and UCF. 

Marshall comes in with a 14-16 record, looking for a win as well, to help their confidence. The Thundering Herd has picked up one Power 4 win, against ACC opponent Virginia Tech.   
  
On the call for the Mountain State Showdown will be Brett Berner, with Matt Foley and Connor Roberts joining him as the Color Analyst.  
  
Ben Stautzenbach will host Pre and post-game coverage. He will be joined by Aaron Pepling, Kam Tomasetti, and Crystal Hanes

First Pitch is set for 6:30; pregame will begin at 6:00. Catch the action on 91.7 FM, [U92themoose.com](http://u92themoose.com), Alexa at home device, and the Tune In App.

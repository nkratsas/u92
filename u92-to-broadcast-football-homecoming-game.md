      WVU Football is looking to find momentum in its Homecoming battle against
      Cincinnati at noon Saturday, Oct. 17, at Milan Puskar Stadium. The
      Mountaineers are 0-3 in Big 12 play and 3-3 overall.


      Third-year quarterback Michael Hawkins Jr. has led the way in his first
      season in the old gold and blue. The Oklahoma transfer has tossed 1055
      yards through the air with seven touchdowns and has rushed for 672 yards
      on 117 attempts for a nation-leading 12 touchdowns.


      A player to watch for Cincy is running back Cole Tabb, who averaged 9.7
      yards per carry. Tabb has a team-high six rushing touchdowns in 2026.


      WVU’s defense has given up 40-or-more points in each game during
      conference play. Despite the offense putting up 30-plus in all but one
      contest, the team sits at .500.


      This game will be the second Big 12 game ever broadcast on U92 airwaves,
      with Nico Caretto bringing play-by-play coverage and Connor Roberts
      joining as the color commentator.


      Matt Foley will host the pregame show starting at 11 a.m. from Milan
      Puskar, while being joined by Conor Bratchford and Cody Burton. You can
      listen to the broadcast on 91.7 FM in the Morgantown area,
      u92themoose.com/listen or on the RadioFX or TuneIn apps.

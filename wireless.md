If you are a long-time listener of U92, you will likely have some familiarity with the station's long-running on-again-off-again mini-zine, Wireless. 

  

Wireless was one of the crown jewels of the station during the pre-internet days, but publication ceased for a period of years until being revived and published intermittently over the years, including in 2009, 2017 and 2019. Now, the station will be able to update with published work throughout the year, including music reviews, sports content, written content to accompany our podcasts and much more. All of this content is student created, making this the first and only time you will hear from me, the students' broadcast adviser.

  
We hope you enjoy our end-of-year content for 2020. We'll keep this updated throughout the year.

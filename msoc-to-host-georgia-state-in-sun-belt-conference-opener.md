WVU is set to host the Georgia State Panthers at Dick Dlesk Soccer Stadium Thursday, Sept. 17, at 7 p.m. The match is the Mountaineers first Sun Belt Conference match this season.

WVU enters 3-1-1 overall and is coming off a 5-0 victory over the Denver Pioneers. Fourth-year forward Pablo Pozos netted two goals and tallied two assists in the contest. 

Pozos leads the squad in goals with four in five matches played, while third-year midfielder Isaac Scheer leads the team with three assists.

Georgia State enters at 5-1-1 overall coming off a 3-0 loss to Wofford. The Panthers are led by midfielder Brandon Graham and defenders Sandro Rabarivony and Kenny Yedmel, who each have scored two goals this season.

The Panthers are a physical team as they have tallied 16 yellow cards already this season, including a season high of four against Lipscomb on Sept. 2.

U92 will provide live coverage, with Quinn Robie providing play-by-play commentary and Brett Berner as the color analyst. The pregame, halftime, and postgame coverage will be hosted by Anthony Ritchie along with Dom Brusco and Gavin Wheeler.

No. 18 WVU Women’s Soccer will take on the No. 20 BYU Cougars Thursday at 7 p.m. at Dick Dlesk Soccer Stadium in Morgantown, West Virginia, in the squad's annual Pink Out game.

WVU enters at 7-1-2 following a 1-0-0 week with a 2-0 victory over Houston. The Mountaineers are led by third-year foward/defender Sophia Nickel with five goals, and fourth-year midfielder Anna Hauer with four assists.

WVU is No. in the country in the RPI. RPI is the NCAA’s top criteria for postseason rankings. WVU is 2-1-1 in Big 12 conference play and is fifth in the standings.

The Cougars enter the match 7-2-2 and are on a four-match unbeaten streak. BYU is led by forward/midfielder Emma Hamberlin with four goals, and midfielder Afton Perry, who has tallied two goals and two assists.

BYU is No. 18 in the RPI rankings. The Cougars are still unbeaten in conference play with a 3-0-1 record and are tied for the conference lead with 10 points.

U92 will provide live coverage, with Quinn Robie providing play-by-play commentary and Cody Burton as the color analyst. The pregame, halftime and postgame coverage will be hosted by Blake Fuchs along with Ethan Farrell, Ryan Boynes and Jonathan Raines.
